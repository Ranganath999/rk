📖 Tips for Better Results

🎯 Be Specific

Include details about style, colors, lighting, and composition. Instead of "a cat", try "a fluffy orange cat sitting on a windowsill, soft morning light"

🎨 Mention Art Style

Add style keywords like "digital art", "oil painting", "photorealistic", "anime style", or "watercolor" to guide the aesthetic

💫 Use Quality Terms

Include terms like "highly detailed", "4K", "professional", "cinematic lighting" to improve image quality

🚫 Use Negative Prompts

Specify what you don't want: "blurry", "low quality", "distorted", "ugly" to avoid unwanted elements
